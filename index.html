Frequently asked

Before you book.

Do I need my exact birth time?

For a detailed Vedic astrology reading, an accurate birth time is strongly preferred because even small differences can affect houses and timing.

Can we discuss a specific question?

Yes. In fact, a specific question and your real-life context can make a consultation considerably more useful.

Do you only predict the future?

No. A consultation can focus on patterns, timing, decision-making, strengths, challenges and possible periods of change — not just future events.

Is astrology a guarantee?

No. Astrology should be treated as a system of interpretation and guidance, not a guarantee of outcomes. Your choices and circumstances still matter.
